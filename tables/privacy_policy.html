2. Your tournament data stays on your device

Everything you enter into the app — tournament names, team names, scores, dates, locations and referees — is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted, never stored on a server, and is not accessible to anyone else. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Because of this, deleting the app deletes your tournaments permanently, and they cannot be recovered.

3. Usage statistics in the app

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how it is used, so that problems can be found and features prioritised. The following is collected:

DataExamples
App instance identifier A randomly generated ID assigned to your installation of the app
Device and system Device model, iOS version, language, approximate country
Usage events App opens, screens viewed, session length, actions such as creating a tournament or adding a team
App state Number of tournaments, teams and games you have; selected colour theme; app version
Diagnostics Errors while saving or loading data, including error codes and available free disk space

Team and tournament names are never transmitted. They are treated as personal data, because teams are often named after real people, and they stay on your device.

This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous: it is tied to a random identifier for your app installation rather than to your name, but it remains personal data under the GDPR.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used, in finding and fixing defects, and in deciding which features to build, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You have the right to object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 GDPR — see section 9.

Recipient: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC in the United States. Retention: 14 months.

4. Feedback sent from the app

The About screen has a Feedback form. If you use it, the message you write is transmitted to a private channel on Telegram. The same channel receives automatic technical reports when data migration fails, which contain error descriptions but no tournament or team names.

The feedback form is anonymous — it carries no identifier and cannot be replied to. Please do not include personal details in it. If you want an answer, use email instead, which is covered in section 6.

Legal basis: your consent through the voluntary act of sending the message, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Recipient: Telegram FZ-LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

5. Donations

Donations are made through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment and billing data is processed entirely by Apple; it is never received here. Only the aggregated fact that a purchase completed is visible, with no identifying details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles that data.

6. Contacting by email

If you email the address in section 1, your message, your email address and any details you include are processed for the purpose of answering you. The legal basis is legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) where your message concerns the use of the app. Messages are deleted once the matter is settled and no retention obligation applies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise any of these, email the address in section 1. Note that analytics data is tied to a random installation identifier, so to act on a request it may be necessary to ask you for that identifier — otherwise your records cannot be located.

To stop analytics: turn off Share usage data in the app's Settings. Collection stops immediately, the analytics identifier already stored on your device is deleted, and the setting stays off after restarts. Deleting the app has the same effect.
